Selling in a Down Cycle: What the Numbers Actually Tell You

James Tuttiett - December 2025 - 4 min read

When the market turns, most sales organisations do one of two things. They either cut costs and wait for conditions to improve, or they double down on activity and hope that volume compensates for conversion. Neither is a strategy.

The sales leaders who come out of a down cycle ahead are the ones who use the compression to do things differently.

What a Contracting Market Actually Tells You

A difficult market is the best diagnostic tool a sales leader has. When budgets tighten and clients scrutinise every spend, the things that survive are the things that genuinely deliver value. Everything else gets cut.

That means if you are still winning in a down cycle, you are winning on merit. And if you are losing, the market is telling you something honest about your proposition that it was politely ignoring when conditions were good.

The UK Numbers in Context

In H1 2025, we operated in one of the toughest professional services markets in recent memory. The UK business limited revenue decline to 14% year on year, while comparable markets declined by 31% to 50%. We won 15 of 21 new clients secured across the entire global group.

Those numbers did not happen because conditions were favourable. They happened because the team stayed close to clients, was honest about what was changing, and adapted the conversation faster than the competition.

What I Would Tell Any Sales Leader Right Now

  • Get closer to your best clients. Not to sell them more, but to understand what is keeping their leadership awake.
  • Be honest about where your current model is under pressure. Clients respect that more than a polished pitch that ignores reality.
  • Identify the two or three things you do that are genuinely hard to replicate. That is where you double down.
  • Measure yourself on new logos, not just retained revenue. Growth in a down cycle is the clearest signal of commercial health.

The cycle will turn. The question is whether you will be in a stronger position when it does.
